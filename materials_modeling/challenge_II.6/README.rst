Graphene electronic properties
===============================
Convergence test, cell relaxation and electronic properties were computed and found inside the ``class_exercise`` folder.

Different K-points
-------------------
All calculations used the same script (``electronic_properties_graphene.py``), editing the K-points parameter. The electronic properties & DOS calculations were repeated using the following K-point grids:

* (12, 12, 1): ``electronic_properties_graphene_12.out`` , ``dos_12.out`` & ``total_dos_12.dat``

* (18, 18, 1): ``electronic_properties_graphene_18.out`` , ``dos_18.out`` & ``total_dos_18.dat``

* (20, 20, 1): ``electronic_properties_graphene_20.out`` , ``dos_20.out`` & ``total_dos_20.dat``

Results were plotted and are shown in ``dos_comparison.png`` .
